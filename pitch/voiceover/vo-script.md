# SkillMap Pitch — Voiceover Script (~40s)

**Read:** confident, conversational, slight emphasis on **bold** words. Pauses marked ⏸.

---

## Script

**[Scene 1 — Hook · 0–5s]**
Your resume gets six seconds of attention. ⏸ Then it's gone.

**[Scene 2 — Problem · 5–12s]**
Every team says they hire *skills*, not resumes. ⏸ Then they scan for keywords.

**[Scene 3 — Product · 12–25s]**
SkillMap reads your real work — your code, your projects, your writeups — and turns it into a verified skill graph. ⏸ What you've proven. Where you're strong. ⏸ Exactly what to learn next — matched to real job demand.

**[Scene 4 — Proof · 25–33s]**
Recruiters get ranked shortlists backed by *evidence*, not keywords.

**[Scene 5 — CTA · 33–40s]**
SkillMap. ⏸ Stop guessing who's qualified.

---

## Notes
- ~72 words → ~30s speech + pauses ≈ 38–40s final
- Word-level timestamps come from edge-tts → drive caption animation
- Voice candidates: `en-US-AndrewNeural` (warm/confident), `en-US-AvaNeural` (expressive), `en-US-EmmaNeural` (cheerful)
